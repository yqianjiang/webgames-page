How Tile Connect Works

Tile Connect is a classic matching puzzle. Your goal is to remove every tile by connecting identical pairs.

  • Select two identical tiles that can connect directly or through a path with no more than two turns.
  • Paths move horizontally and vertically; diagonal connections are not allowed.
  • Clear every tile to complete the level.
  • Choose from multiple themes and difficulty levels.
  • Use hints and shuffles when you need help finding the next pair.

The game is free and requires no download. Open it in a phone, tablet, or desktop browser and start playing.

Scoring

Combo bonus

Match more pairs within 3 seconds of one another to build a combo and earn increasing bonus points.

Level-clear bonus

Unused hints and shuffles award 50 points each, every remaining second awards 10 points, and each level adds a fixed level-number × 100 bonus.

Tile Connect FAQ

What is Tile Connect?

Tile Connect is a matching puzzle where you remove identical tiles by linking them with a path that makes no more than two turns.

How do I play?

Select two identical tiles. They disappear when a valid path with at most two turns can connect them. Clear every tile to win.

What is the three-line rule?

The connecting path can contain at most three straight segments, which means it may turn no more than twice.

When does a game end?

The game ends when time runs out or no valid pairs remain, even after an automatic shuffle.

How do hints and shuffles work?

A hint highlights a removable pair. Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles to help create new matches.
